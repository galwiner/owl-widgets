# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People learning optics through experiments in Python notebooks. They adjust a system, inspect its behavior, and learn by changing parameters and observing results.

## Product Purpose

OWL is a reusable, extensible in-notebook optics bench for teaching. Success means learners can build and modify optical systems, see meaningful results immediately, and reuse the widgets in their own notebooks.

## Brand Personality

Practical, clear, exploratory. Keep prose minimal and let experiments and plots teach.

## Anti-references

Avoid opaque black-box widgets, sprawling prose-heavy lessons, and interfaces that pull learners away from notebook experimentation.

## Design Principles

- Keep experiments interactive and close to their controls.
- Make widgets reusable and extensible across notebooks.
- Keep models inspectable; make important behavior visible in plots.
- Prefer concise labels and direct visual feedback over instructional prose.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard interaction and visible focus, readable contrast, reduced-motion preferences, and color-vision deficiencies. Do not rely on color alone to communicate plot meaning.
